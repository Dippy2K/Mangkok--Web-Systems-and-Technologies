Who We Are

At Mangkok, we bring you the Filipino flavors you crave—fast, fresh, and unbelievably affordable. Our commitment is simple: Sarap to the max, presyong chill! We specialize in classic Pinoy favorites served in convenient, hearty bowls.

Try our bestsellers like Pork Sisig, Beef Caldereta, Pritong Chikee, and Pork Belly. Whether you need study fuel, a quick barkada lunch, or a satisfying late-night craving, Mangkok has a delicious bowl waiting for you.

Our Vision

To be the leading source of high-quality, authentic, and affordable Pinoy comfort food across the metro.
